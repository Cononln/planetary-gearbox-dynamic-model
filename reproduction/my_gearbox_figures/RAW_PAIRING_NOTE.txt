Raw response pairing update (2026-09-19)

The previous *_fault_component figures used (fault - healthy) residuals.
That makes the healthy layer identically zero by definition and exaggerates
the visual difference between healthy and severe crack cases.

The *_raw_paired figures now use the original mean-removed DTE response from
each time_*.csv with one common Z scale. No model parameter, TVMS value,
crack depth, or signal sample was changed.

The existing spec_*.csv files are the direct one-sided FFT of these raw
responses. The spectrum figures were re-exported from those unchanged files,
so their numerical amplitudes are unchanged and are paired with the raw time
figures.
