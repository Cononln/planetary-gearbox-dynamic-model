ZCode time/frequency figure pairing notes (2026-09-19)

1. Spectrum figures
   The existing spec_*.csv files and the four spectrum plotting scripts are
   unchanged.  The spectra are the one-sided FFT of the corresponding raw
   time_*.csv DTE response after mean removal.

2. Time-domain fault-component figures
   The new *_fault_component figures display

       fault component = fault response - healthy response - linear trend.

   No amplitude scaling, smoothing, interpolation, or artificial impulse is
   applied.  Peak-preserving display decimation retains the local minimum and
   maximum in every time bin so narrow pulses remain visible after export.

3. Interpretation
   The time-domain figures are diagnostic fault-feature-component plots.
   The spectrum figures are spectra of the original full DTE response.
   They are complementary views and are not a direct FFT pair.

4. Numerical consistency audit
   19/19 time/spec pairs passed.  N = 172032, duration = 2 s,
   Fs = 86016 Hz, df = 0.5 Hz, spectrum range = 0...1999.5 Hz.
   Across all pairs, the maximum reconstructed amplitude error was
   9.05e-19 micrometre and the minimum spectral correlation was
   0.9999999999999998.
